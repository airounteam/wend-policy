# Privacy Policy for Wend

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Airoun Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides Wend (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App or contact us.

## 1. Photos and content on your device

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with us. The nickname you enter is stored locally on your device.

With your permission, the App reads the photos and videos on your device to find your trips. It reads when and where each photo or video was taken (date and location metadata), shows thumbnails, and computes simple image values (brightness and a similarity fingerprint) to group similar shots. All of this happens on your device. Trips, place names, best shots, the photos you add to or remove from trips, and these computed values are stored locally on your device. Place names come from a city database built into the App, so locations are not sent anywhere to look up names. We do not operate a server to receive or store your photos, videos, or this information.

You can choose to give the App access to all photos and videos or only to selected ones, and you can change this at any time in your device settings. Denying access prevents the App from finding trips but does not give us access to anything else on your device.

The App never deletes photos on its own. When you choose to clean up photos, Android shows its own confirmation, and approved photos are moved to your device's trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.

The App may ask for notification permission to show progress while it finds trips. You can manage this permission in your device settings.

Android backup and device-transfer features may copy App data according to your device settings and backup provider's practices. Local storage does not mean that these system-managed backups are disabled.

## 2. Usage analytics and crash reports

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, which send usage and diagnostic information to Google. These services do not receive your photos, videos, their location data, or your nickname. The App does not display advertisements, but using these services means that the App is not free of data collection.

### Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Analytics to understand App usage and improve the App. It processes information such as App interactions, sessions, device and App information, and App-instance identifiers. IP addresses are used to derive approximate location. Android advertising identifiers may also be collected depending on device availability and settings. These identifiers distinguish App installations or devices; they are not the same as a named account with us.

See Google's [Analytics data disclosure information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727).

### Firebase Crashlytics

We use Crashlytics to investigate crashes and improve reliability. It processes crash traces, diagnostic information, device and operating-system information, App versions, and installation identifiers. Analytics events may appear as diagnostic breadcrumbs in crash reports.

See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 3. Support emails

If you contact airoun.team@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your name, message, and attachments. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and address the issue you report. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We delete support correspondence and associated personal information without undue delay once the inquiry has been resolved and the information is no longer needed for that purpose. Where applicable law requires retention, we retain the required information for the legally required period.

## 4. Service providers and international processing

We use Google's Analytics, Crashlytics, and Gmail services for the purposes described above. Information processed by these services may be handled on servers outside your country, including in the United States. System-managed backups are handled by the backup provider you use.

Google's handling of information is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the applicable service terms. Google's [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) also describes its international data-transfer arrangements. We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Retention

- **Local App data:** Your nickname, trips, and other App data remain on your device until you delete them with "Delete Wend data" in the App's settings or clear the App's storage. Uninstalling normally removes local App data, but system backups may remain and must be managed separately. Deleting App data never deletes the photos and videos on your device.
- **Analytics:** Our event-data retention setting is 2 months, and our user-data retention setting is 14 months. Reset on new user activity is enabled, so new activity renews the applicable user-data retention period. These settings do not limit most standard aggregated reports. See [Google Analytics data retention](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196).
- **Crashlytics:** Google retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion from live and backup systems, as described in its Firebase privacy information.
- **Support correspondence:** We follow the deletion rule in Section 3.
- **System backups:** These are subject to the backup provider's retention practices and your account settings, rather than the App's local deletion controls.

## 6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can delete the App's data with "Delete Wend data" in the App's settings, clear App storage through Android settings, and manage photo, notification, and backup permissions through your device or provider settings. Clearing storage or uninstalling the App does not itself request deletion of information already sent to Google.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, object to processing of, or receive a copy of your personal information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact airoun.team@gmail.com. We do not maintain a mapping between support email addresses and Analytics or Crashlytics installation identifiers. An email address alone therefore does not allow us to locate a particular installation's records. We will explain any identification limitations and assess requests under applicable law; this does not remove your statutory rights. We will not ask you to create an App account to submit a privacy request.
